The story
changed shape.

Before

A story to complete

Turning point

Try it. Then rethink it.

After trying that direction, the design changed: keep the meal as it was and let the story become optional.

Recorded in the dated spec and pivot commit
→

After

A postcard to discover

A swinging “Click me!” postcard opens three illustrated rooms—Bari, Soho and Charlotte Street—with a gentle hide-and-seek, then returns to a shared table.

Shipped interaction · no quizzes or forced steps
Opt-in curiosityOffer a small invitation; let the guest decide to follow it.
History beside imaginationKeep sourced context distinct from fictional scenes and play.
Quiet resonanceLet the story deepen the meal without taking it over.
