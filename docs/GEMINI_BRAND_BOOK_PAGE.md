# One-page brand-book plan — Human × Gemini

**Format:** 1920×1080 landscape spread for the brand book. This is a copy/layout plan, not a claim that a Canva presentation has been exported.

**Headline:** Made in conversation.

**Subheading:** Human direction. Gemini-led drafting and critique. A workflow grounded in a working café prototype.

## Main copy

We brought an existing Italia Uno game and the Creative Track brief to Gemini. Its first draft focused too narrowly on a brand-book summary. Our feedback redirected the work toward the full toolkit, four hand-ins and the decisions behind the experience. Gemini reorganized the narrative; we then required clearer evidence labels and corrected unsupported technical claims.

The result is a reusable workflow connecting the customer journey, drawn space, optional postcard game, menu, owner simulation, sound and publishing. The conversations document this review and reconstruction—not the historical authorship of every line of code.
