React, React DOM, Vite, the official React Vite plugin, Tailwind CSS, the Tailwind PostCSS plugin, TypeScript and type packages are installed through npm. Their own license files are included with installed packages; pinned versions are recorded in `package-lock.json`.

Three.js belonged to an earlier hosting prototype and is not used or redistributed in this standalone edition. Referenced games and open-source management projects informed high-level design only; no source or assets from them are copied. The original score contains no third-party recording, sample or transcribed melody.
